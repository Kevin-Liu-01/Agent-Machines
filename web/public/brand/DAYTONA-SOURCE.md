# Daytona glyph

Source: [Daytona's official brand kit](https://www.daytona.io/brand), retrieved September 9, 2026.

Original SVG: https://framerusercontent.com/images/zEFS9o7MWBdO4bXzDSIDr3euk.svg

`services/daytona.svg` retains the official glyph path geometry. The presentation-board background is omitted, the viewBox is cropped around the glyph with clear space, and the monochrome fill is black. The application renders this SVG as a `currentColor` mask for legible light and dark themes. Daytona's brand guidelines permit the glyph where a full logotype would not fit, such as these compact provider controls.

The mark identifies the third-party sandbox provider. It does not imply endorsement or affiliation.
